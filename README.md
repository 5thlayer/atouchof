# A Touch Of

A suite of Minecraft 26.1.2 / NeoForge survival mods inspired by Don't Starve. Each mod brings one
danger into an ordinary world and plays alone in vanilla.

A Touch Of is a fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Klei Entertainment, the makers
of Don't Starve.

## The suite

- **A Touch of Darkness**: the Dark strikes anyone who stays unlit, and every light burns out.
  It comes first.
- **A Touch of Madness**: Sanity, Shadow Creatures that only the mad can see, and Umbra.
- **A Touch of Wickedness**: killing harmless creatures builds Wickedness, and too much of it
  brings the Bailiff to seize your goods.

Cold and heat are left to [Tough As Nails](https://modrinth.com/mod/tough-as-nails), which the suite
recommends and no Pillar requires; A Touch of Cold is shelved.

No Pillar requires another. Together they meet in vanilla state: the Dark drains Sanity because
Madness reads light level, not because the two mods know each other.

## This repository

This is the suite's home: the shared vocabulary, the decisions that touch more than one Pillar,
and the parent issue of every cross-Pillar topic.

- The vocabulary is in [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md), and the decisions are in [docs/adr](docs/adr).
- A bug or idea for one Pillar goes on that Pillar's tracker. One that spans several goes here.
